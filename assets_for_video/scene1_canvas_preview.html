Cell Structure & Function

▸ The Cell — Basic Unit of Life

All living things are made of cells
→ Two types: Prokaryotic & Eukaryotic
Prokaryotic = no nucleus (bacteria)
Eukaryotic = has nucleus (plants, animals)

▸ Key Organelles ★

Nucleus — "Control center", contains DNA
• Nuclear envelope = double membrane
• Nucleolus → makes ribosomes
Mitochondria — "Powerhouse" → ATP production
• Equation: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ATP
Ribosome — protein synthesis (rough ER surface)
Golgi Apparatus — packages & ships proteins
Cell Membrane — phospholipid bilayer ← semi-permeable

▸ Plant vs. Animal Cells

Plant cells ONLY have:
✓ Cell Wall ✓ Chloroplasts ✓ Large vacuole
Animal cells ONLY have:
✓ Centrioles (cell division)

▸ Transport Across Membranes

Passive (no energy): Diffusion, Osmosis
→ High conc. to low conc.
Active (needs ATP): pumps ions against gradient
★ Exam tip: osmosis = WATER only!
